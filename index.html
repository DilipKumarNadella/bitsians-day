The Story

One family. Many time zones. Endless nostalgia.

Every year, the BITS Pilani Alumni Association brings together graduates from Pilani, Goa, Hyderabad, Mumbai and Dubai for a single, global celebration. From redi runs and wing legends to convocation memories, BITSians' Day is where the campus comes to you.

Pick your city on the map, RSVP through your local volunteer, and walk into a room full of people who instantly get every inside joke.

📍 Register on bitsaa.org and update your location so your local POCs can keep you posted about events near you.
